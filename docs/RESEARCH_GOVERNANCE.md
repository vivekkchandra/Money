# Research governance

Money ends at research. No broker account, positions or order interfaces are permitted. Trading212 is an eligibility provider only.

First-pass workers receive only a frozen ResearchMandate and ResearchSnapshot. They have no report repository, peer reports, shared conversational memory, qualitative Qlib inputs or CIO conclusions. Persistence occurs outside the firm capability. Trusted adapters must restrict upstream tools to the snapshot; production process/network isolation must additionally prevent upstream plugins bypassing this boundary.

All three report identities and snapshot hashes must match, each report must be persisted once, and a database transaction must lock the barrier before LEAN, final CIO audit or cross-examination can consume them. A failed or missing firm prohibits normal consensus. At most two cross-examination rounds are accepted. Unresolved material contradictions suppress signals.

CIO verifies claims against objective evidence, including recomputation where deterministic. Red Team only looks for failure; VETO is final. Eligibility, ethics, PIT, freshness, conflict, mandatory LEAN and audit gates override confidence. Independence counts original evidence families and source/model/provider/argument overlap, never repeated LLM agreement.

Research states are STRONG_RESEARCH_CANDIDATE, RESEARCH_CANDIDATE, WATCH, REJECT, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE and EXPIRED. The aspirational £1,000 objective is excluded from gate calculations. Outcomes measure published research independently of user trading decisions.
